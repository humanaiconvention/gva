# GVA-1 collection decision

Recorded 25 September 2026 UTC, before any GVA-1 confirmation outcomes were generated.

The maintainer elected to make independent review packages available and proceed with the next research steps without waiting for external review. The [review package](GVA1-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) is ready. Independent review remains outstanding and will be reported as such.

This changes the sequencing of the rough research roadmap, not the scientific protocol. External review completion is not a criterion in the frozen preregistration. The protocol, all eighteen bounds, effect margins, calculated sample of 145 roots, seed schedule, execution source and runtime remain unchanged. No confirmation outcome was used in this decision.

Collection will use the published `gva1-execution-v0.1` seal, all roots 4000001..4000145 and all sixteen paired cells. Monitor completion counts only; perform the frozen analysis after every root is committed and audited. Preserve all conditions, bounds, audit results, costs and any interruption/deviation evidence. Do not tune the optimizer, change physics, add roots or select repairs based on intermediate outcomes.

The next design stage is a separately committed transfer study. Confirmation results may inform its explicitly developmental design, but will not be reused as fresh transfer evidence. Independent GVA-0 replication and GVA-1 review remain open alongside that work.
