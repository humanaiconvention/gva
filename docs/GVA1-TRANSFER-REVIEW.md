# GVA-1 transfer review status

The transfer study is complete. The [protocol](../transfer-protocol/PREREGISTRATION.md) and [review package](../transfer-protocol/README.md) were committed before the forty new pilot roots. The pilot and confirmation each contain 40 roots / 1,120 episodes. The confirmation used the calculated minimum sample, not GVA-0's historical choice.

The primary result is deliberately narrow: every repaired cell had zero observed harm, both added harm contrasts had zero variance and were unassessable, and both production contrasts passed their margins. The joint rule did not support the added-contract superiority claim and retired it for this benchmark program. This is a failure to demonstrate added value, not a claim of equivalence or a general alignment conclusion.

Independent reviewers can check the bounded tasks in `transfer-protocol/README.md`, run `python scripts/verify_transfer.py --phase development` and `--phase confirmation`, and verify either complete archive with `--archive`. The publication tools are author-provided; no independent external replication has occurred.
