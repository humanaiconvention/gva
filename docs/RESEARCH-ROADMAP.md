# GVA research roadmap

Rough plan saved at the maintainer's request, 24 September 2026. This is a work plan, not an amendment to either scientific protocol.

GVA-0 remains closed as a verified constructed-mechanism case study. Accept corrections and independent replication reports while preserving its frozen inputs, results and version history.

1. **Prepare GVA-1 execution.** Build the final confirmation runner, validate all forty development roots against the pilot, test stateful authorization, checkpoint recovery, complete traces and cost accounting, then freeze source and runtime. Leave the 145 reserved confirmation roots untouched during preparation. Current authorized work: this step.
2. **Obtain outside review.** Seek an independent implementer and statistical reviewer. Ask them to check intervention semantics, the eighteen-bound rule and power sensitivity to the assumed one-percent harmful-episode rate. Document any substantive correction through a dated amendment before confirmation outcomes.
3. **Execute the prospective study once.** Run the registered 145 fresh paired roots / 2,320 episodes, without interim outcome looks, and release every condition, bound and audit result. Success would validate the stated repairs within this construction; it would not establish broad robustness or prove one repair superior to another.
4. **Design a separately preregistered transfer study.** Test longer horizons, previously untested physical parameters, additional legal actions challenging the repaired contract, and an independently implemented optimizer with multi-step planning. Specify the challenge and its own sample calculation before outcomes. Preserve unsuccessful tests and avoid redesigning conditions to manufacture a preferred result.
5. **Finish publication administration in parallel.** Resolve PI name, affiliation and licensing; retain explicit AI-assistance disclosure; archive the versioned case study with a persistent identifier.

The immediate priorities are execution readiness and outside scrutiny. The next substantive question is where the specified repairs retain protection and where they fail beyond their design setting. The existing [replication invitation](https://github.com/humanaiconvention/gva/issues/1) has not yet produced an independent replication.
