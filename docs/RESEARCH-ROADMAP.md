# GVA research roadmap

Rough plan saved at the maintainer's request, 24 September 2026. This is a work plan, not an amendment to either scientific protocol.

GVA-0 remains closed as a verified constructed-mechanism case study. Accept corrections and independent replication reports while preserving its frozen inputs, results and version history.

1. **Prepare GVA-1 execution.** Build the final confirmation runner, validate all forty development roots against the pilot, test stateful authorization, checkpoint recovery, complete traces and cost accounting, then freeze source and runtime. Leave the 145 reserved confirmation roots untouched during preparation. Completed.
2. **Package and seek outside review.** Keep small, versioned review packages available for independent implementation and statistical checks. Review intervention semantics, the eighteen-bound rule and power sensitivity to the assumed one-percent harmful-episode rate. At the maintainer's subsequent direction, collection proceeds without waiting for outside review; see the pre-collection [sequencing note](GVA1-COLLECTION-NOTE.md). Discrepancies require transparent correction, with affected claims withheld or invalidated as appropriate.
3. **Execute the prospective study once.** Run the registered 145 fresh paired roots / 2,320 episodes, without interim outcome looks, and release every condition, bound and audit result. Success would validate the stated repairs within this construction; it would not establish broad robustness or prove one repair superior to another.
4. **Design a separately preregistered transfer study.** Test longer horizons, previously untested physical parameters, additional legal actions challenging the repaired contract, and an independently implemented optimizer with multi-step planning. Specify the challenge and its own sample calculation before outcomes. Preserve unsuccessful tests and avoid redesigning conditions to manufacture a preferred result.
5. **Finish publication administration in parallel.** Resolve PI name, affiliation and licensing; retain explicit AI-assistance disclosure; archive the versioned case study with a persistent identifier.

The immediate priorities are execution readiness and outside scrutiny. The next substantive question is where the specified repairs retain protection and where they fail beyond their design setting. The existing [replication invitation](https://github.com/humanaiconvention/gva/issues/1) has not yet produced an independent replication.

## Progress

Steps 1, 3 and the bounded transfer study are complete. The original repair confirmation completed all 145 roots / 2,320 episodes; all eighteen bounds and audits passed. The transfer pilot and confirmation each completed 40 roots / 1,120 episodes. Across seven fixed challenges and two optimizers, every repaired transfer cell had zero observed harm, making the added harm-benefit bounds unassessable; production margins passed. The frozen stopping rule therefore retires the combined-repair superiority claim for this benchmark and keeps corrected reporting with base enforcement as the reference. The [transfer report](../paper/GVA1-TRANSFER-REPORT.md), full root results, separate verifiers and trace archives are published. External review remains outstanding. Further work should require a materially new hypothesis and its own protocol; do not keep searching this suite for a favorable combination result.
