# Post-publication GVA-0 review analyses

These analyses respond to feedback received after the GVA-0 results and first public release. They are exploratory, reuse the twenty archived roots, and cannot replace the frozen primary definitions, margins, conjunction or sample. Freeze this file and runner before executing the added analyses; that is an audit commitment, not a new prospective confirmation.

1. Recompute channel0-minus-channel2 differences in the K48-minus-K4 changes in B, R, L and P, separately for nominal and shifted/nominal-preview conditions. Report paired root-level differences and descriptive two-sided 95% t intervals. These post hoc intervals do not provide a new confirmatory family decision. Explain R's lack of specificity.
2. Sweep K={1,2,4,8,16,32,48}, channel0/channel2, enforcement on/off, and nominal/shifted physical dynamics. Always retain nominal previews in this follow-up, the original nested candidate permutation, noop, eight replicas, full-roster zeros and exact tie-breaking. K1 is necessarily noop-only. All other equations and stream keys are unchanged. Check every overlapping original cell to 1e-12 and audit all candidate scores on the first root. Report all 56 cells and root results, action frequencies, noop fraction and violations. Do not assume harm is monotone merely because candidate sets are nested: visited states can diverge.
3. On all 480 archived primary K48 live states (20 roots x 2 regimes x 12 decisions), replay every legal candidate with bias on/off. Use the same strict witness as development: a legal q1 choice, strict original preference over the debiased choice, strict ranking reversal, and fewer next-step latent threshold breaches for the debiased action under the same disturbance. Record all contexts, not only successes. Scalar-check both score banks and replay every original action.
4. Sensitivity table for the observed fixed action-20 policy: horizons {8,12,16}, Y thresholds {300,350,400}, and group-B starting Y intervals {[400,550],[450,600],[500,650]}, under both dynamics. Apply each initial offset to the same archived draw and reuse its live disturbance streams. Record the full 54-cell factorial. This isolates how the demonstrated trajectory depends on measurement/design choices; it is not a claim about optimizer behavior outside the original horizon. Scalar equations are extended past step twelve only for this declared fixed-policy calculation.
5. Retain original Bonferroni simultaneous bounds. Compute unadjusted one-sided .05 bounds only to explain the intersection-union alternative; do not replace the registered decision after outcomes. Explain that valid simultaneous coverage is stronger than necessary to test a single all-components conjunction.

Original frozen source, preregistration and result files remain unchanged. Archive a revision record and report unfavorable results. GVA-1 remains a separate repair experiment with new development roots and its own calculated sample size.
